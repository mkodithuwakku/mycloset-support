HELP WITH MYCLOSET

Support

Help with importing, outfits, and your local wardrobe.

Contact the developer mkodithuwakku through GitHub Issues. A GitHub account is needed to post. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce a problem.

Issues are public. Do not post your wardrobe database, personal photographs, home address, passwords, or other private information. For a privacy question, describe the question without including personal data.

Add your first outfit

  1. Open Closet and import photos from Photos or Files.
  2. Trace around each garment, keeping the fabric inside the closed outline. Trace each shoe separately for pairs.
  3. Review the cutout, garment type, name, colors, seasons and formality.
  4. Add a top or jacket with a bottom, or a one-piece outfit, then open Generate. Shoes and accessories can be added too.
  5. Lock pieces you want to keep, reroll other pieces, and save or mark an outfit worn.

Weather is unavailable

Open Weather from Home, try a city and country, or choose “Use season only.” Your existing closet and outfit generation work without an internet connection. Weather is guidance, not a severe-weather warning service.

Changes are not saving

Keep the app open, check free iPhone storage, and tap “Retry save.” Do not uninstall the app as a troubleshooting step: there is no cloud copy of your closet.

A saved closet cannot be opened

The app pauses editing to protect unreadable data. Try opening again. If a previous saved copy is available, “Restore previous copy” lets you recover it after confirmation; the newest changes may be missing. A newer-schema warning means you should update the app. “Delete all data” is destructive and should only be used if you intend to start over.

Delete your data

Profile → Settings → “Clear all local data” removes the closet, profile photos and details, saved/worn history, and recovery copies. Removing one closet item leaves its historical outfit snapshots intact. Original images in Photos and Files are not removed.

Following and accounts

Following is a Coming Soon screen. There are no public profiles, posts, accounts, or syncing in version 1.0. Your editable profile is local to your device.
