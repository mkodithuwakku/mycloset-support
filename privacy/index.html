Privacy policy

Your wardrobe stays in the app. Optional weather makes limited network requests.

Who provides the app

myCloset is provided by mkodithuwakku. For support or privacy questions, use the support page. No myCloset account is created.

Data on your iPhone

The app stores the clothing photos you select, prepared garment cutouts, item descriptions, colors, categories, seasons, availability, favorites, your local profile and profile photo, and saved/worn outfit history in its app container. Image analysis and outfit generation run on-device. These records are not uploaded to the developer or an AI service, sold, used for advertising, or used to train a cloud model.

The Photos picker gives the app the images you choose; Files import gives it the files you select. The app prepares new image pixels for storage and removes source EXIF and GPS metadata. It does not modify your original Photos or Files library.

Optional weather

When you choose “Use my location,” the app requests foreground location permission. Before contacting Open-Meteo, it rounds latitude and longitude to a 0.1-degree grid (roughly 11 km north/south). It does not send exact coordinates, closet content, a profile, or an app-generated user identifier.

When you enter a city, your search text is sent to Apple for geocoding. Enter a city and country rather than a home address. The resulting coordinates are rounded before the weather request. Apple processes geocoding under its privacy policy.

Open-Meteo receives rounded coordinates and your network IP address. Its privacy policy says technical and troubleshooting logs can include IP addresses and coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. These requests provide weather and support service reliability and abuse prevention. Weather responses are cached only in app memory for up to 15 minutes.

You can use the app without weather or location permission. Choose “Use season only” in Weather to stop pending work and clear the in-memory weather cache and city text. Already transmitted requests cannot be recalled. Revoke location permission in iPhone Settings at any time. The app does not request background location or track you across apps.

Retention, deletion and recovery

Local records remain until you remove them or clear the app. Saved and worn outfits are independent snapshots: removing a closet item does not remove its photo from past outfits. A previous saved copy and, after recovery, an unreadable original can remain locally to help recover a damaged closet. Individual changes may remain in these copies until later saves overwrite them.

Profile → Settings → “Clear all local data” removes the closet, images, profile, history and local recovery copies. If deletion is interrupted, the app completes it when reopened. The app never intentionally reports an incomplete deletion as successful. Deleting the app may also permanently remove your data; offloading an app is different and can retain data.

There is no app-managed backup, cloud sync, or cross-device recovery. Version 1.0 excludes its storage directory from new operating-system backups. This does not erase backups made by older versions or copies you manage outside the app. For old device backups, use Apple's backup-management controls. The developer cannot recover your private wardrobe.

Diagnostics and support

The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Apple may provide crash, usage, or TestFlight feedback under your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy. If you choose to contact support through GitHub, we use the information you submit to answer the request. GitHub Issues are public: do not include personal photos, precise locations, passwords, or private wardrobe files. You can edit or delete your GitHub contributions using GitHub's controls, subject to its retention policies.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts. GitHub processes website requests and support interactions under the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes and choices

Material changes to the app's data handling will be reflected here with an updated date. You can decline weather, revoke location access, remove local content, or stop using the app. For questions, request contact through the public support channel without posting sensitive information.
